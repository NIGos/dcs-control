## The scale (Part A and Part B)

| Key | Where was the sound? |
|---|---|

| **1** | at my head, on its surface |

Front or back (Part A): **Up** = in front of me, **Down** = behind me. Guess when unsure.

A/B/X: A, B and X play in turn. X is the same as A or as B. **1** = X is A, **2** = X is B.
