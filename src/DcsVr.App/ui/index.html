What Pupil shift does and does not do

  • Does: keeps the cockpit still against the outside world when only your eyes move.
  • Does not: fix lens blur, distortion at the edges or sharpness.
  • It follows your eyes up to about 35° from the center.

Diagnostic package

One ZIP file with what is needed to understand a problem: attach it to your report on the forum or GitHub. Create it right after the flight that went wrong, before starting DCS again (DCS starts its log anew).

Updates

DCS Control asks GitHub for a newer release at most every 6 hours. Nothing is downloaded until you choose Update, and only a setup signed with DCS Control's release key is installed.

What the package contains

  • This app's report: the profile, what was detected on this PC, the checks and the last flight.
  • DCS: dcs.log, options.lua, autoexec.cfg, the engine optimizations' log and reports, the versions of the DLSS and DirectX files in its bin folder.
  • The logs and settings of DLSS 5 (Cheeky), frame generation (OFXR), Quad Views, Pupil shift and CPU Boost.
  • Pimax Play's runtime and service logs and settings, or SteamVR's on the Sboys route.
  • The OpenXR runtime and every OpenXR layer registered on this PC, Windows version, CPU and GPU with its driver.

Only text files, each cut to its last 8 MB. Your Windows user name, PC name and e-mail addresses are replaced by <user>, <pc> and <e-mail>, and the account tokens, account ids and headset serial number in Pimax's logs are removed too. Nothing is sent anywhere: the file is saved where you choose.

In-headset panel messages

What the lines of the in-headset panel mean.

DLSS 5: ON but not applied
DLSS 5 is on, but no image reached it. After the session, DLSS 5 on the focus area (on this page) explains the reason shown in brackets.
DLSS 5: status not updating
DLSS 5 stopped reporting: DCS may be loading or paused.
Engine Opt.: 12 of 17 on
Some engine optimizations are not working. Normal for a few seconds after a mission loads; if it lasts, the Engine Optimizations page shows which ones. With a graphics driver that cannot record draws on other threads (common on AMD), Multi-threaded G-buffer and Multi-threaded shadows always show as off: they are not supported there.
Engine Opt.: none on
Engine Optimizations are installed but none of them works: see the Engine Optimizations page.
Pupil shift: no gaze
No eye-tracking data reaches Pupil shift: Quad Views is off, Focus movement is Fixed, or the headset's eye tracking is not running. See the Perception page.
