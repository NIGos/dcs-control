Test suite

Measures the optimizations off and on inside DCS, in alternating blocks for several minutes, and writes a report. Start it in a flight (in VR, keep the headset on and awake). In DCS, Ctrl+Alt+F11 starts or stops it too.

What it corrects, and what not

  • Corrects the perspective of near objects when only your eyes move: the cockpit no longer shifts slightly against the outside as you look around (about 0.2° at 70 cm with 25° of gaze).
  • Corrects the slight slide of the whole image against the lens's image as the pupil moves.
  • Does not correct the lens's own distortion and blur beyond about 25° of gaze, or sharpness: those belong to the lens.
  • The focus area follows your gaze up to about 36°; beyond 35° the correction stays at its 35° value. During a fast eye movement the focus may move ahead for an instant ("Focus ahead of fast eye movements"), while vision is suppressed anyway.

Diagnostic package

One ZIP file with what is needed to understand a problem: attach it to your report on the forum or GitHub. Create it right after the flight that went wrong, before starting DCS again (DCS starts its log anew).

What the package contains

  • This app's report: the profile, what was detected on this PC, the checks and the last flight.
  • DCS: dcs.log, options.lua, autoexec.cfg, the engine optimizations' log and reports, the versions of the DLSS and DirectX files in its bin folder.
  • The logs and settings of DLSS 5 (Cheeky), frame generation (OFXR), Quad Views, Pupil shift and CPU Boost.
  • Pimax Play's runtime and service logs and settings, or SteamVR's on the Sboys route.
  • The OpenXR runtime and every OpenXR layer registered on this PC, Windows version, CPU and GPU with its driver.

Only text files, each cut to its last 8 MB. Your Windows user name, PC name and e-mail addresses are replaced by <user>, <pc> and <e-mail>, and the account tokens, account ids and headset serial number in Pimax's logs are removed too. Nothing is sent anywhere: the file is saved where you choose.

Panel messages

What the in-headset diagnostic panel's lines mean.

DLSS 5: ON but not applied
DLSS 5 is on, but no image reached it. The reason in brackets is explained above after a session.
DLSS 5: status not updating
DLSS 5 stopped reporting: DCS may be loading or paused.
Engine: 12 of 17 on
Some engine optimizations are not working. Normal for a few seconds after a mission loads; if it lasts, the Engine Optimizations page shows which ones. With a graphics driver that has no D3D11 command lists (common on AMD), the two multi-threaded recorders always show as turned off: they are not supported there.
Engine: none on
The engine optimizations are installed but none works: see the Engine Optimizations page.
Pupil shift: no gaze
The profile or the headset gives no eye-tracked focus area: see the Pupil shift page.
